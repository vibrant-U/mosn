Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our community. If you don't find what you're looking for, contact us directly.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email and in your account dashboard. Use the "Track Order" feature in your account or enter the tracking number on our partner courier websites.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Contact customer support to initiate a return and get a free return label if eligible.

How secure are payments on MOSN?

All payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant gateways with SSL encryption. We use trusted providers like MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, and Visa/Mastercard for safe transactions.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location: 1-3 days in Kampala, 3-7 days nationwide. Express options are available for faster delivery. Check product pages for estimated times.

Can I cancel my order?

Orders can be canceled within 1 hour of placement if not yet processed. After that, contact the seller directly through your order details page for cancellation requests.
