About MOSN Marketplace

Connecting Ugandan businesses and consumers in a trusted, innovative marketplace

Our Mission

MOSN Marketplace is Uganda's premier multi-vendor e-commerce platform, inspired by the success of Alibaba. We connect local businesses with customers across the country, providing a trusted space for buying and selling goods and services.

Founded in 2025, our platform serves as a bridge between traditional retail and modern e-commerce, making it easier for Ugandan entrepreneurs to reach more customers and for consumers to access quality products from verified sellers.

Why Choose MOSN?

We prioritize trust, quality, and convenience. Every seller on our platform is verified, and we provide secure payment processing, fast delivery options, and comprehensive customer support. Whether you're looking for electronics, fashion, agricultural products, or home goods, MOSN Marketplace has you covered.
